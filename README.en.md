# 🌊 BADA : Get Your Score

> **A free English exam-prep web app for self-directed students**  
> **BADA** (*meaning "sea" and sounding like "receive" in Korean*) represents navigating toward your goals while getting the score you worked for.

### 🌊 [Open the BADA web app — bada-study.vercel.app](https://bada-study.vercel.app)

No install, no sign-up. It runs right in your browser (iPhone·iPad Safari and desktop).

---

## 🎯 Why BADA?

Built to solve two major hurdles students face when studying for school English exams on their own without private tutoring or paid workbooks:

1. **Lack of practice variations**: Never having enough modified questions for the same reading passages.
2. **No feedback on writing**: Struggling to grade and improve sentence ordering, topic sentences, and summary writing alone.

BADA requires no sign-up and no paid API keys. Any student can connect it with the free AI they already use (ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude) to generate new questions and get instant feedback.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **12 Practical Exam Question Types**
  - Multiple Choice: Main Idea, Title, Vocabulary, Grammar, Blank Inference, Ordering, Sentence Insertion, Summary, Irrelevant Sentence, Implied Meaning
  - Writing & Study Tools: Phrase Ordering, Topic/Summary Writing, Blank Passage Recall, Sentence Structure Analysis (S/V/Modifiers)
- **3 Difficulty Levels**: Easy, Standard, and Hard tailored to your study stage.
- **Zero-API AI Integration**: Generate and copy a structured prompt in one click, run it in your own AI chat, and paste the JSON response back into the app.
- **Study History**: Automatically saves your solved questions, mistakes, and feedback locally for review.

---

## 📖 How to Use

### 1. Practice Built-in Questions
1. Go to the **`Practice`** or **`Question Set`** tab and choose your passage, question type, and difficulty.
2. Solve the questions and check instant answers, explanations, and writing feedback.

### 2. Add Unlimited Questions with Your AI (`AI Generator` Tab)
1. Open the **`AI Generator`** tab and select your AI (**ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude**), question type, and difficulty.
2. Click **`Generate & Copy Prompt`** and paste it into your AI chat.
3. Copy the AI's JSON output and paste it into **`Import JSON`** inside the app to start solving right away.
   *(Note: It is completely normal if the AI response looks like a code block.)*

### 3. Review Your Progress
- Check the **`History`** tab anytime to revisit past attempts and review mistakes.
